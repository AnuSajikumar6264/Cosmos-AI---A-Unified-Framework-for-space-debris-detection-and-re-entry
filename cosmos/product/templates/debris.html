Methodology, dataset provenance and limitations

Dataset. SpotGEO v2 (ESA / University of Vienna challenge): sequences of five 640×480 grayscale frames with annotated GEO object positions; the test split of 5,120 sequences is used (manifest data/feature2_debris/spotgeo/MANIFEST.json). Frames carry a frame index, not a UTC time, and no catalogue identity.

Methods evaluated. Classical COSMOS front end (frame differencing + difference-of-Gaussians, hard detections) and heat-map U-Net detectors trained from scratch or with an ImageNet ResNet-18 encoder on difference and high-pass channels; scored with the challenge's official F1 / MSE scorer (outputs/benchmarks/vision_spotgeo/). X14 re-scored the archived per-sequence submissions by difficulty stratum (outputs/spotgeo_difficulty/).

Findings. U-Net F1 0.746 ± 0.005 (3 seeds), ResNet-18 encoder 0.765 ± 0.034 (2 seeds), classical front end 0.084; the front-end ablation (raw high-pass only, 0.751) is negative for the registration front end.

Limitations. Pixel positions without astrometric calibration cannot be converted to sky coordinates or associated with any catalogued object; no confidence or timestamp exists for the served detector; benchmark scores are not operational validation.

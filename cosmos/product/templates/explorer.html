What this view is. Each ellipse is one catalogued object's real orbit size, shape and tilt, computed from the archived perigee altitude, apogee altitude and inclination of the {{ provenance.as_of }} snapshot. The snapshot publishes no ascending node, argument of perigee, epoch or anomaly, so the orientation of each orbit about the pole and the position of each marker on its ellipse are a deterministic screen layout, not a measurement. Nothing is propagated, no position at any time is shown, and no ground track is drawn, because the archive cannot support one (limitation P5). Only the camera moves; no object is animated along its orbit.

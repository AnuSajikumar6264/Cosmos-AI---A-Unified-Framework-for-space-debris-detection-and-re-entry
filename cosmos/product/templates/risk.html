Methodology, dataset provenance and limitations

Dataset. ESA Kelvins Collision Avoidance Challenge: anonymised conjunction data message (CDM) time series for 13,154 training events (8,293 kept after filtering) and a held-out split of 2,167 events, 150 of them above the 1e-6 high-risk threshold (outputs/benchmarks/conjunction_kelvins_cac/summary.json). Event ids are positional indices; no NORAD identity exists in the data.

Method. Quantile regression of log10 collision probability from the last CDM's features (learner selected out-of-fold among tabm, tabicl and kumo; kumo best), with the {{ evidence.model }} reliability gate. Metrics: pinball loss on the predicted quantiles and conditional coverage by risk stratum. Positioned against the challenge's published baselines (X12).

Finding. The gate's mean test weight is about 0.0002, so the gated fusion contributes almost nothing: a negative result anchored to the public benchmark. Conditional coverage fails in the high-risk stratum, where it matters.

Limitations. Benchmark predictions on archived events; not a validated collision-avoidance capability; no event can be attributed to a catalogued object; health evidence never changes a physical probability (coupling C4 abstains).

An operational ordering, never a probability or a risk estimate — full scope and limitations
What the ordering is and is not. The priority score is an operational queue computed by the rule recorded in the run manifest, read here rather than reimplemented. It is not a probability, not a risk estimate and not a collision warning. It uses the point 30-day probability and the catalogued mass, never the uncertainty interval. Its usefulness has been measured on one archived, non-blind window only (below).

Standing limitations of this screen

  • the ordering uses the point probability and the GCAT mass, not the interval;
  • mass is unknown for many fragments, and the multiplier is then never applied;
  • no health or conjunction evidence enters the ordering (C4 abstains);
  • the only effectiveness measurement is the non-blind window above;
  • a prospective, blind evaluation is blocked until a catalogue snapshot dated on or after 2026-11-26 exists.

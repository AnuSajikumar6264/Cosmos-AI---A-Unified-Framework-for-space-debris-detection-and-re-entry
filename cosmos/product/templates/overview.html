Evidence boundary. Every figure below is read from a saved COSMOS-AI artifact or a dated catalogue snapshot. This console does not track satellites live, and a dated snapshot is never presented as real-time. Software tests passing is not scientific validation.
What this system is today. COSMOS-AI is a read-only evidence twin: no model runs at prediction time, every value is read from an archived evaluation or the dated catalogue snapshot, and no live telemetry, tracking or conjunction-message feed is connected. Telemetry, conjunction and imagery evidence are independent benchmark roots that cannot be attributed to any catalogued object; they are not combined into a satellite health assessment.
Evidence classes. This console serves archived real-data evaluations only (anonymised ESA-ADB telemetry, Kelvins conjunction events, SpotGEO frames, the CelesTrak/GCAT snapshot). The project's synthetic demonstration data — simulator telemetry and rendered imagery of the legacy chain — is not shown here and is never mixed with real-data evidence.

How to read the globe. Orbit size, shape and tilt are archived values; orientation about the pole and each marker's place on its ellipse are a labelled screen layout. No position at a time, no ground track, nothing propagated, no object animated in time — only the camera moves. Full view and geometry basis.

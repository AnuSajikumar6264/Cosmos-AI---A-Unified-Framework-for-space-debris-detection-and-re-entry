Uncertainty and abstention how to read the intervals

lifetime intervalMondrian conformal quantile regression, stratified by object class and perigee band, calibrated on a held-out fold. Not a distribution-free guarantee for a single object.
re-entry interval5–95 % Monte-Carlo spread over area-to-mass and space-weather draws. Not a conformal interval.
zero width13,765 objects: every draw returned the same value. This is the integrator's discretisation, never confidence.
at the cap14,028 objects sit at 1826.25 days: "not expected within the cap", not an estimated lifetime.
abstention18,158 objects have no lifetime value and 123 have incoherent per-horizon probabilities (L17). Both abstain; neither is filled with a default.

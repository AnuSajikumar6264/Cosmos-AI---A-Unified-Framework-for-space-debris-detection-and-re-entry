Lifetime prediction coherence

Rows reaching lifetime_estimates.csv are monotone across horizons. The saved per-horizon calibrated columns p_lifetime_30d/90d/1yr are produced by an independent isotonic calibrator per horizon and are not guaranteed to form a coherent CDF (limitation L17); the twin's final reported p_* forecast is unaffected.

2 object(s) in this run were excluded for exactly this reason and are listed in lifetime_noncoherent_horizons.csv.

Collision risk and operational priority

collision_risk.csv holds the physical collision probability of each anonymised CAC event with a 0.05-0.95 interval; its priority_score is deliberately empty. Operational ordering lives only in decision_ranking.csv and never alters a physical probability.

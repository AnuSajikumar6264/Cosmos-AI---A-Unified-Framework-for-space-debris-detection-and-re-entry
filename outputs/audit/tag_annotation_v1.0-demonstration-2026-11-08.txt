What this release is: a read-only, archive-backed satellite digital twin and its Mission Intelligence Console, serving
one dated catalogue snapshot (2026-10-27, 31,396 objects) with lifetime, re-entry, decision-priority and twin-state
evidence, plus three independent research benchmarks (ESA-ADB telemetry, Kelvins CAC conjunctions, SpotGEO debris)
that are never attributed to a catalogued object. No live data source is connected and no model runs on demand:
every value is an archived prediction re-expressed through typed contracts with provenance, uncertainty and abstention.

Known limitations are stated on every screen and in docs/product/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md: anonymised telemetry and
conjunction data, constant space-weather features, no cross-satellite generalisation or health-to-conjunction fusion,
disagreeing re-entry variants, 123 incoherent L17 lifetime records, archived (non-prospective) validation only,
point-probability priority, no persisted model weights. Author-only manuscript fields, licence and data DOI are not
part of this release. Thesis DOCX/PDF require an external toolchain; the Markdown draft is included.
